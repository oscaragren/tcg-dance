Card pool simulator (prototype)

Question: which Card pool and Upgrade model should the relaunch use? Each model below starts from the real game on 9 Nov 2026: today's remaining pool and the Copies held by the 40 players who made a Daily claim in the last week (anonymised). Simulated players buy Packs at today's rate (about 195 a day in total, shared out by how much each player already plays). They always buy from the Collection they are furthest from finishing, and they Upgrade only their spare Copies, never their last Copy of a Card.

Throwaway prototype. Chest Cards (about 9% of Cards today), Trades and Diamonds are left out. "Complete" means owning every Card in a Collection except the Specials.
